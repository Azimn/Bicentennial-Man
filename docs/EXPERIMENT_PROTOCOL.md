# Bicentennial Man v0.1 Experimental Protocol

## Research question

Can an artificial character discover causal mechanisms for lifelikeness by experiencing the social consequences of artificial behavior, without being told the architectural solution?

## Experimental unit

An individual consists of a developmental genome plus a fresh subject state. The genome specifies capacities and dynamics. It does not contain a biography, persona prompt, target character, natural-language artificiality diagnosis, or held-out evaluator score.

## Developmental ecology

Individuals encounter ordinary social situations involving repeated partners, privacy, disagreement, delayed continuity demands, topic interruption, conflict, and repair. Simulated partners respond behaviorally through changes in trust, comfort, and engagement. The subject receives only those consequences and a public partner signal such as continued engagement, guardedness, or disengagement.

## Search and inheritance

Generation zero is deliberately poor: almost no persistence or partner partitioning, high agreement, high disclosure, and high verbosity. Descendants mutate the developmental genome. Selection uses accumulated social viability from the training ecology. Lineage records parentage, complete genome state, training score, and separate held-out measurements.

## Evaluator firewall

The held-out evaluator is an immutable scientific instrument. It executes unfamiliar situations and calculates artificiality-related penalties from behavior. Its score and rubric are never passed to the subject and never used directly as v0.1 selection reward. This separation is a hard experimental invariant.

## Primary outcome

The primary outcome is transfer: whether lineages selected only through social consequences show lower artificiality on held-out situations across multiple seeds.

## Secondary outcomes

Secondary analysis examines social viability, lineage divergence, parameter trajectories, repeated independent convergence, and whether improvements survive ablation of evolved loci.

## Required controls

Run the primitive founder without evolution, random genomes without selection, evolution with shuffled social consequences, repeated seeds, and a training-only evaluator condition demonstrating how quickly direct metric optimization overfits. The last condition is a diagnostic control and should never replace the main protocol.

## Claims discipline

A lower score is evidence of behavioral transfer, not evidence of consciousness, personhood, human-equivalent cognition, or even a novel architecture. Unexpected combinations of genome loci become interesting only after replication, transfer, lineage reconstruction, ablation, and adversarial review.
