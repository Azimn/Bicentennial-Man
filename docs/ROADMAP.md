# Research Roadmap

## v0.1: Consequence-driven genome search

Establish the evaluator firewall, deterministic lineage, primitive founder, social ecology, bounded mutation, held-out transfer measurement, and adversarial review workflow.

## v0.2: Controls and ablations

Add random-search, shuffled-consequence, no-development, direct-metric Goodhart control, multi-seed replication, locus ablation, and lineage replay.

## v0.3: Structural mutation

Replace the fixed vector-only genome with generic component slots that can be added, removed, duplicated, connected, and assigned resource cost. Preserve provenance for every structural mutation.

## v0.4: Population ecology

Introduce multiple social partner phenotypes, changing networks, reputation, third-party observation, resource constraints, and novel held-out villages. Prevent any single critic population from defining the target.

## v0.5: Developmental histories

Freeze promising evolved architectures and expose them to different biographies. Compare same-origin divergence and target-history reconstruction, including Pretorius Canon as a separate experimental layer.

## Later: constrained source synthesis

Only after structural evolution is interpretable should an external coding model propose sandboxed modules. Generated changes must be isolated, diffed, tested, reversible, resource-bounded, and subject to the same held-out and ablation requirements.
